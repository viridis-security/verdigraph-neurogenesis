How is this calculated?
  • Cache lift: each percentage point of cache-hit-rate increase saves ~80% of the per-task cost for those tasks (cache cost is near-zero).
  • Local-GPU shift: each percentage point of frontier-to-local rerouting saves the cost difference between the two tiers (frontier ~50× local).
  • Retry reduction: each percentage point of retry-rate reduction directly reclaims that fraction of compute.
  • Token volume estimated from spend assuming weighted avg $0.012 per 1k tokens for frontier, $0.0015 for mid-tier; local share converted at $0.04/kWh ÷ frontier_kwh_per_1k.
  • CO2e equivalents from EPA Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator (2024 factors).
  • This is a planning estimate, not an audit. Verdigraph's developmental ledger is what produces auditable savings reports.
